# Asset rights, provenance, and launch use

## What the included assets are

The media in this package was collected from the public portfolio pages explicitly linked in the supplied project materials. It is included as a practical working set for layout, CMS seeding, crop testing, motion behavior, and an authorized New Work build workflow.

Public visibility is **not** proof of an open license, ownership transfer, client approval, talent release, music clearance, or permission to republish. Before a public launch, New Work should confirm its right to use each file on the new domain and replace portfolio-compressed copies with approved masters wherever possible.

## Status conventions

- `prototype-only`: safe for internal build/layout work; not approved for public launch by this package.
- `owner-review`: the relevant New Work contributor should confirm project identity, selection, credit, and reuse rights.
- `replace-with-master`: the included public rendition is compressed, derived, or incomplete and should be replaced.
- `do-not-publish-without-explicit-approval`: a test, ambiguous client association, or other heightened caveat.

All collected assets should be treated as `prototype-only`, `owner-review`, and usually `replace-with-master` until New Work records a stronger status.

## Specific caveats

- Oliver's protected Vimeo films were not bypassed or copied. Where direct film extraction was unavailable, the package uses public page posters/stills and records the Vimeo reference. Obtain an approved embed or master from Oliver.
- Michael's Squarespace page provides portfolio-resolution renditions, three GIFs, one directly hosted video, and seven YouTube embeds. The included MP4 loops are working preview derivatives, not source masters.
- Several Michael labels are inferred from filenames. The Chanel-labelled still is explicitly a test and must not be published under that client without approval.
- The source pages do not provide definitive copy, credits, dates, client mappings, alt text, captions, transcripts, or clearances for every item.
- Three Anjali portfolio references supplied in the original brief (Adobe, Stella Artois, and Rakuten) are packaged as prototype-only working images. They remain explicitly blocked from production until Anjali confirms the selection, metadata, credits, reuse rights, and approved masters.
- The creative-reference deck and design-site screenshots are research inputs, not public site assets.
- No commercial font files are included. The identity designer must provide font files together with web-use license confirmation.

## Launch clearance checklist

For every project selected for publication, confirm:

1. Project title, client/commissioner, year, role, contributors, and credits.
2. Permission to publish each still, loop, poster, film embed, logo, person, and soundtrack on the New Work site.
3. Approved source master and color/crop direction.
4. Written alt text and, where applicable, captions/transcript.
5. Public visibility, intended home order, and social-share crop.
6. Whether an external player may be embedded and whether domain/privacy settings are configured.
7. Any expiry, territory, award-entry, test-work, or client-review restrictions.

Record that decision in Sanity's internal rights fields or the production asset tracker; do not rely on this ZIP as the final clearance record.
