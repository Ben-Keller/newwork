## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Edit target: the attached existing New Work Agency compact favicon. Create ONE refined favicon variation with more breathing room for the letters, keeping the same recognizable two-row, diagonal n / w arrangement and the same restrained brand character.

Preserve: lowercase heavy grotesk n at the upper left, heavy lowercase w at the lower right, warm ivory lettering and rectangular blocks on a near-black charcoal rounded-square tile. Flat, crisp, vector-like artwork. Two horizontal rows, not a full wordmark.

Change: the current letters are crowded by solid blocks. Make the n and w more clearly legible by giving each letter a wider silhouette and generous open counters; shorten the upper-right and lower-left solid blocks so a clear, deliberate charcoal gap separates each block from its adjacent letter. Widen the horizontal charcoal gap between the two rows slightly. Keep consistent outer padding of about 12% of the tile. The two letters should remain bold enough to read at 16 and 32 pixels. Think optical-size logo refinement, not ornament.

Composition: centered square icon only, large on the canvas, transparent outside the softly rounded charcoal tile. Exact text: n and w only. No labels, extra letters, shadows, gradients, mockups, texture, perspective, borders or presentation sheet. Preserve the black-and-ivory palette and visual balance.
