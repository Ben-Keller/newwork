## Required project record

A project is eligible only when all of these are true:

- title, unique slug, short description, project type, role, year, contributors, and credits are confirmed;
- cover and every content block have approved assets, meaningful reviewed alternatives (or an intentional decorative choice), crops, and source dimensions;
- meaningful video has captions and/or a useful transcript; provider URL and player ID agree exactly;
- project rights status is **Approved**, approval evidence is recorded, and any expiry remains in the future;
- each asset has provenance plus asset-level approval evidence, terms/territories, approval date, and expiry where applicable;
- all review, prototype, placeholder, master, embed, and explicit-approval blockers are cleared deliberately;
- `visible`, `featuredOnHome`, `homeOrder`, publication date, SEO title/description, and approved social image/alt are reviewed;
- the published page is checked in protected preview before the production build is released.

Changing `visible` or publishing a Studio document is never sufficient on its own. GROQ and the content adapter independently recheck the safety contract.

## Site-wide owner inputs

- Final full wordmark, compact/social mark, favicon, SVG specifications, palette, motion direction, licensed WOFF2 files, and web-font license evidence.
- Approved About copy; approved names, roles, biographies, project-owner associations, and optional candid/portrait image with alt; approved contact email; social destinations; optional manifesto; expertise.
- Approved default SEO title, description, 1200×630 share image and alternative; optional About/Contact overrides.
- Decision and privacy approval for analytics. It remains disabled unless production mode, Studio approval, and a token are all present.
- Decision on Reel and Notes. Missing or disabled content remains absent without orphan headings or invented copy.

## Release sign-off

The owner/editor, rights approver, and release operator should each record their decision in the release ticket. A release is blocked by any expired approval, unresolved placeholder/master/embed, failed production build, failed browser/accessibility check, or unverified deployed headers/canonicals.
